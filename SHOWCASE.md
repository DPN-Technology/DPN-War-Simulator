## Product Snapshot

**Current release:** v2.7  
**Publisher:** DPN Technology  
**Primary focus:** 3D simulation · First-person gameplay · Ships · Vehicles · Open-world systems  
**Development state:** Active

DPN War Simulator is being developed as a cohesive simulation platform with continuous traversal, interactive environments, ship and vehicle systems, combat, damage, AI behavior, and scalable world simulation.

## Core Value

- Seamless ship, deck, room, and world traversal goals
- First-person interaction and combat
- Ship and vehicle physics
- Damage, AI, crew, and world-simulation systems
- Production-quality 3D asset pipeline
- Automated Python simulation tests
- Automated 3D dependency and asset-pipeline validation
- Controlled releases, SBOMs, checksums, and supply-chain evidence
