# v1.5 Persistent Carrier Air Wing

The v1.5 air wing expands carrier aviation beyond the earlier package-level launch buttons. Aircraft and aircrew now persist as individual simulation records.

## Squadrons

The training air group contains VF-6 (F4F), VB-6 (SBD), VS-6 (SBD) and VT-6 (TBD). Squadron labels and aircraft categories reflect Enterprise-era carrier aviation. Exact individual roster names/counts are training abstractions in this release.

## Individual aircraft

Aircraft track:

- Aircraft type and training bureau number
- Squadron
- Condition
- Fuel
- Ammunition
- Ordnance
- Hangar/Flight/Air status
- Assigned pilot
- Flight hours
- Sorties

## Aircrew

Aircrew track experience, fatigue, morale, injuries, availability and mission count.

## Mission cycle

1. Select squadron.
2. Select CAP / Scout / Strike / ASW / Ferry.
3. Select sortie size.
4. Plan the flight.
5. Serviceable aircraft are individually spotted to the Flight Deck.
6. Launch requires safe deck condition and adequate wind over deck.
7. Missions burn fuel and accumulate flight time while airborne.
8. Scout missions can transmit role-limited training reports.
9. Flights transition to RETURNING and must be recovered.
10. Aircraft return to Hangar status and can require servicing.

## Stores

Air-wing missions consume aviation fuel and, for strike missions, bombs or torpedoes. Maintenance consumes spare parts and aviation fuel. The quantities and rates are gameplay calibration rather than exact archival CV-6 inventories.
