# USS Enterprise CV-6 — v2.7 Midway 1942 Reconstruction

## Target
The reconstruction is locked to Enterprise around the Battle of Midway, late May / early June 1942. The purpose of v2.7 is to increase human-scale ship fidelity while maintaining a strict boundary between source-supported visual facts and reconstruction.

## Source-constrained / photo-constrained direction
The reconstruction uses official Navy/NHHC period photographs for visible island, mast/radar, crane, director, searchlight, loudspeaker and weapon-fit cues. The Yorktown-class 1940 CV-5 Booklet of General Plans is used as a class-level arrangement constraint for major deck relationships, the long open hangar and elevator relationships.

Primary reference catalog identifiers are recorded in `historical_data/enterprise_1942_reconstruction_v27.json`.

## Exterior work completed in v2.7
- 251.4 m overall lofted hull target with fine/raked bow and cruiser-stern cues.
- 244.6 m flight-deck target with stained wood treatment, tie-down-strip texture, centerline/distance markings, arresting wires and barriers.
- Three elevator assemblies.
- Open-sided framed hangar with transverse frames/trusses, gallery belts, curtains, ventilation trunks and lighting.
- Faceted island / pilot-house massing with bridge glazing and wings.
- Tapered rectangular funnel, tripod mast and CXAM-style radar mattress.
- YE beacon cue, aircraft crane, searchlights, loudspeakers and Mk.33 director cue.
- 8 single 5in/38 mounts, 4 quad 1.1in mounts and 30 modeled 20 mm positions as the Midway-period art target.
- Catwalks, railings, safety-net lattice, LSO platform, galleries and sponsons.
- Boats/life rafts, anchors/chains, shafts, propellers, rudder/skeg.
- Deck tie-down eyes, arresting-gear housings, barrier stanchions, fire lockers, hose reels, capstans and ready lockers.
- Hull plating/scupper visual cues and reconstruction-only paint-boundary cues.
- Island access doors, porthole cues, external ladders, bridge-wing rails and signal halyards.
- Representative F4F/SBD/TBD deck aircraft with better fuselage, wing, canopy, gear and folded-wing geometry.

## Interior reconstruction modules
### Hangar
Includes structural frames/beams, elevator rails, fire-curtain tracks, ventilation, aviation fire stations, workbenches, lighting and tow tractors.

### Bridge / Pilot House
Includes forward glazing, helm wheel/column, engine-order telegraphs, chart table, voice tube, annunciator panel, compass binnacle, pelorus stands, mechanical dial bank, seats and access door.

### CIC
Includes central plot, radar/scope consoles, status board, battle-phone racks, stools, cable trays and red battle-light cues.

### Engineering / Machinery
Includes representative boilers, turbines, generators, auxiliary pumps, piping, valve wheels, gauge boards, catwalks, ladders, floor gratings and lighting.

## Accuracy boundary
The exterior is increasingly photo/class-plan constrained. Hidden interior equipment placement is still reconstruction. v2.7 does not claim exact Enterprise-specific machinery-room pipe routing, exact CIC console coordinates, exact bridge furniture placement, exact hull plate seams, exact deck-fitting coordinates, or exact 1942 paint RGB values.

The goal for future passes is to replace each reconstruction-only area as archival Enterprise-specific material becomes available, without changing the overall simulation architecture.
