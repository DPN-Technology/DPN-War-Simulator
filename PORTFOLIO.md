## DPN Repository Baseline

DPN projects should maintain product documentation, architecture and installation guidance, security policy, contribution workflow, issue templates, CI validation, release controls, roadmap/release history, supply-chain evidence where applicable, and product-specific branding.

Production credentials, private databases, keys, backups, and operational exports must not be committed.
