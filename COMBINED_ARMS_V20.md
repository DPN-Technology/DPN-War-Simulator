# War Simulator v2.0 — Combined-Arms Ground Combat

v2.0 adds a first-person infantry/fireteam combat layer to the same seamless world that already contains Enterprise, the naval task force, the carrier air group, bases, expeditionary airfields and the campaign system.

## Historical / simulation policy

The combined-arms training village, opposing-force units, weapon effects, cover values, squad names, support effects and casualty coefficients are **TRAINING / GAMEPLAY ABSTRACTIONS**. They do not rewrite the locked Battle of Midway historical scenario and are not presented as archival WWII small-arms or ground-combat data.

## Seamless training village

The Naval Training Base has been expanded south again. The same `BASE` world layer now includes:

- Infantry Armory / Equipment Issue
- Fireteam Command & Combined-Arms Briefing
- Field Aid / Casualty Collection Point
- Combined-Arms Training Village
- Multiple building interiors
- Sandbag / low-cover lines
- Lane dividers and maneuver corridors
- Observation tower
- Assault checkpoint and objective zones

No scene load is used to enter the training village.

## Player infantry state

The player now has combat-specific:

- Health
- Stamina
- Suppression
- Standing / crouched / prone stance
- Aim state
- First-aid kits
- Weapon heat / fire cooldown
- Wounded status
- Shots / hits / damage-taken statistics

Movement speed changes with stance, suppression and sprint stamina.

## Weapons

Training weapon classes:

- Service Rifle
- Automatic Rifle
- Service Sidearm

Every weapon has a finite magazine, reserve ammunition, reload time, effective training range, accuracy, damage and suppression values. These values are gameplay calibration, not historical test tables.

## Fireteam

A four-person simulated fireteam is physically present in the training area:

- Fireteam leader
- Automatic rifleman
- Rifleman
- Corpsman

Orders:

- FOLLOW
- HOLD
- SUPPRESS
- ASSAULT
- FALL BACK

Fireteam members track health, suppression, morale, ammunition and wounds. They move toward assigned positions and can suppress or assault detected contacts.

## Opposing force / detection

Training contacts begin hidden. Confidence develops from player observation and proximity rather than instantly displaying the entire exercise.

Contacts track:

- Health
- Suppression
- Cover
- Detection confidence
- Identification
- Role
- Status

Player range, view alignment, stance, aim, suppression and target cover influence hit probability.

## Casualties and medical response

The player can perform self-aid with finite first-aid kits. The corpsman can treat wounded fireteam members. The physical Field Aid station remains available in the same world.

## Combined-arms support

Support is finite and connected to systems built in earlier phases:

- Carrier-air support consumes `ground_ops.air_support_points` generated by completed Air Group missions.
- Simulated naval fire support requires an available friendly task-force ship and consumes escort ammunition.

Support therefore cannot be spammed without logistics consequences.

## Exercise objectives

The initial v2.0 field exercise has three sequential objectives:

1. Move to Assault Checkpoint
2. Secure Training Village
3. Casualty Collection / Accountability

Strong performance can award the career qualification:

**Combined-Arms Fireteam Practical**

## On-foot controls during the exercise

- W/A/S/D — move (D remains strafe-right only)
- Shift — sprint
- C — cycle standing / crouched / prone
- Left mouse or Space — fire
- Right mouse — aim
- R — reload
- 1 / 2 / 3 — select weapon
- T — cycle detected target
- J — cycle fireteam order
- H — self aid
- M — treat fireteam casualty
- 5 — request carrier-air support
- 6 — request simulated naval fire support
- F — objective guidance
- G — combined-arms status board
- E — world interaction
- X — stand down from exercise
- Esc — infantry help

## Persistence

v2.0 persists the complete ground-combat snapshot plus career statistics for:

- Exercises completed
- Best score
- Opposing contacts neutralized
- Support calls
- Casualties treated
- Training losses

Existing saves remain backward compatible.
