# USS Enterprise (CV-6) — v2.6 Midway 1942 Visual Reference

## Period lock
Late May / early June 1942, centered on the Battle of Midway.

## Source-driven visual cues used in v2.6

- Full-size Yorktown-class carrier proportions rather than the old compact prototype.
- Wooden flight deck.
- Metal tie-down strips and flight-deck distance markings visible in 4 June 1942 deck photographs.
- Three elevators.
- Starboard island with large integrated stack.
- CXAM-1 masthead radar visible in March 1942 island photographs.
- Searchlights and aircraft crane visible around the island.
- 1.1-inch quad AA mounts in period island photographs.
- 20 mm Oerlikon battery being installed in March 1942 and present in period photos.
- F4F Wildcat, SBD Dauntless and TBD Devastator deck aircraft for the Midway-era air group.

## v2.6 reconstruction boundary

The following are still reconstruction/game-art approximations rather than claimed exact archival geometry:

- precise hull station curves and plating seams
- exact sponson/gun coordinates
- exact bridge window spacing and island dimensions
- paint RGB/roughness values
- internal compartment arrangement
- exact aircraft spotting positions

That boundary is intentional. Future art phases should replace approximated dimensions with traced plans rather than silently inventing details.
